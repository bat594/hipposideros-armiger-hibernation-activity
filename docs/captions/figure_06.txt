Fig. 6. Peak phase and temporal variation in colony-level activity during the monitoring period. (a) Distribution of daily 24-h Cosinor acrophases across 47 complete monitoring days. The shaded band denotes 18:00–24:00; acrophases after midnight are displayed on the following 24–30 h segment to preserve circular continuity. Thirty-eight of 47 daily acrophases (80.9%) fell between 18:00 and 24:00. Orange circles indicate the 10 calendar dates flagged for human disturbance in the review dataset (date-level flags, not counts of cave-entry events); all symbols are plotted at the same size. (b) Temporal variation in daily mean group activity, with a dashed least-squares trend line; the association with monitoring date was weakly negative (Spearman's rho = -0.29, P = 0.048). (c) Temporal variation in the proportion of daily activity occurring between 18:00 and 24:00, with a dashed least-squares trend line; this proportion increased with monitoring date (Spearman's rho = 0.51, P < 0.001). Daily summaries were calculated from the quality-controlled 10-min group activity series.
