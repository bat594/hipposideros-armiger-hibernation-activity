Fig. 2 Validation of the computer vision-derived activity index against manual observations at two temporal scales. (a) Relationship between machine-derived activity load and manual counts in 90 independent 10-s video segments (Spearman's rho = 0.813, P = 2.12 × 10⁻²², 95% CI = 0.700–0.890). (b) Relationship between machine-derived activity load and mean manual counts in 30 independent 10-min windows, using the final averaged manual count as the reference (Spearman's rho = 0.652, P = 9.414 × 10⁻⁵, 95% CI = 0.281–0.904). Dashed lines indicate simple linear fits against log10(machine-derived activity load) and are shown only as visual guides.
