Fig. 7 Behavioural composition and day-to-day variation in Hipposideros armiger during high-activity periods. (a) Overall composition of the five non-resting behavioural categories across the 47 observation days, expressed as percentages of all non-resting observations; values beside bars give percentages and category counts. (b) Daily proportions of the five non-resting behavioural categories relative to all bat-observations recorded on each date; daily sample sizes are shown in parentheses. The heatmap uses a common power-normalized colour scale (gamma = 0.30) to improve visual discrimination of low-frequency categories; colourbar tick labels show the original, untransformed percentages
