Fig. 5. Daily activity patterns of the hibernating Hipposideros armiger colony. (a) Mean 24-h activity profile across 47 complete monitoring days; the shaded band represents the 95% confidence interval obtained by bootstrap resampling of monitoring days. (b) Observed clock-time mean of robust log-transformed activity and the fitted 24-h Cosinor curve. The blue band indicates the 95% day-bootstrap confidence interval, the dashed horizontal line indicates the MESOR, and the dotted vertical line marks the acrophase (19:43). (c) Mean fast activity load across four 6-h clock intervals; error bars represent 95% confidence intervals obtained by resampling monitoring days. (d) Hourly distribution of within-day top-decile 10-min windows across the 47 days, defined as windows at or above each day’s 90th-percentile activity threshold. A total of 388 of 691 top-decile windows (56.2%) occurred between 18:00 and 24:00; the expected proportion under a uniform distribution across the 24-h cycle is 25%.
