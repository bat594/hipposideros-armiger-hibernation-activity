Fig. 3. Date–time distribution of colony-level activity in Hipposideros armiger during the monitoring period. The heatmap shows robust log-transformed 10-min mean activity load, calculated as ln[1 + fast activity load / median positive fast activity load], across 47 complete monitoring days (15 February–6 April 2026). Dates without a complete-day record are omitted. White cells indicate masked or insufficient-coverage intervals within the plotted days. Of 6,768 possible 10-min bins, 6,658 met the coverage criterion (98.4%).
